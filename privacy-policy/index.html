Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

EKPIX Creative Web Studio ("EKPIX", "we", "us") operates the website ekpix.in and the EKPIX Android app (package name in.ekpix.business). This policy explains what information is handled when you use them.

The EKPIX Android app

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

Buttons in the app open other apps or websites on your phone: the dialer, your email app, WhatsApp, Instagram, or your web browser. Anything you do in those services is handled by them under their own privacy policies.

Information you send us

If you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, or the enquiry form on our website, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, and project requirements. We use this only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and deliver the work you ask for. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Website

  • The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering those fonts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

Third-party links

Our app and website link to other sites, including our portfolio projects hosted on Vercel. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other sites.

Data retention and your rights

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to respond and to complete any project. You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information you have sent us by emailing the address below.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
